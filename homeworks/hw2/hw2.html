Hashing, Key Derivation and Cryptographic Primitives

An exploration of the cryptographic standards behind Ethereum wallets, from mnemonic phrases to checksummed addresses, including statistical properties.

01 / INTRODUCTION

Understanding Ethereum Wallets

An introduction to the cryptographic mechanisms involved in Ethereum wallet generation.

From Mnemonic to Address

Ethereum wallets can use multiple cryptographic standards to derive private keys and corresponding public addresses from a mnemonic phrase.

Each stage of this process has a different purpose, including key management, public-key cryptography, hashing and error detection.

03 / CRYPTOGRAPHIC STANDARDS

Standards and Primitives

Understanding the role of each technology in the derivation process.

BIP-39

Mnemonic sentences and seed generation using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512.

BIP-32

Hierarchical deterministic key derivation from a master seed.

BIP-44

Standard derivation paths for multi-account HD wallets.

secp256k1

Elliptic-curve cryptography for public-key generation and signatures.

Keccak-256

Cryptographic hashing used in Ethereum address derivation.

EIP-55

Mixed-case checksum encoding for Ethereum addresses.
